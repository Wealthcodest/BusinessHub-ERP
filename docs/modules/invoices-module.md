# Invoices Module

Invoices will manage estimates, invoices, line items, tax, discounts, payments, due dates, statuses, printable documents, and customer balances. Draft documents may be edited; issued documents require controlled amendment or credit-note workflows.

Build the module around a document header and line-item editor, with totals calculated consistently on client and server. Laravel is authoritative for numbering, tax calculation, payment allocation, status transitions, and business scoping. Future invoice events should feed accounting and reporting.
